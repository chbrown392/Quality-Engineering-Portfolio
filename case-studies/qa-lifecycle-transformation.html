Sanitized Enterprise Salesforce Case Study

Enterprise QA Lifecycle Transformation

Designing a governed path from business intake to production validation.

Context

A complex enterprise Salesforce delivery environment needed a clearer, repeatable quality process across business intake, development, QA, UAT, release management, and production validation.

Challenge

Quality activities existed, but handoffs, entry/exit expectations, ownership, and release gates needed to be made explicit so work could move predictably through the delivery lifecycle.

My Role

QA Lead and Salesforce QA SME responsible for defining the quality operating model, QA handoffs, UAT readiness expectations, test strategy, and release-validation checkpoints.

Approach

  • Mapped the workflow from business intake through closure and metrics.
  • Defined quality gates between refinement, development, QA, UAT, and release.
  • Established clear ownership for ticket movement and validation.
  • Created reusable acceptance-criteria, test-case, defect, and release-readiness practices.
  • Separated business validation (UAT) from technical/functional QA while ensuring both remained connected.

QA / Test Strategy

Testing was aligned to requirements, risk, role/permission behavior, data integrity, regression impact, and production-validation needs.

Result

The organization gained a documented, repeatable quality path that reduced ambiguity around when work was ready to move and what evidence was expected at each stage.

What This Demonstrates

QA governance, Salesforce delivery leadership, lifecycle design, cross-functional handoffs, UAT governance, release readiness, and process maturity.
