Release Readiness Checklist

A focused quality gate used to determine whether a change is ready to progress toward production.

Checklist

  • Acceptance criteria validated.
  • Blocking defects resolved or formally accepted.
  • Targeted regression completed.
  • Role/permission impacts validated where applicable.
  • UAT completed when required.
  • Deployment dependencies documented.
  • Known limitations communicated.
  • Production smoke-validation steps defined.
  • Rollback/escalation ownership understood.
  • Release notes updated.
