# UAT Governance Framework

## Objective
Make UAT a controlled business-validation gate rather than an informal final check.

## Entry criteria
- QA testing complete for agreed scope.
- Blocking defects resolved.
- UAT environment/data ready.
- Business tester and test window confirmed.
- Acceptance criteria and expected outcome available.

## Ownership
- QA: prepares the release for UAT, communicates known risks, supports reproduction, and confirms defect fixes.
- Business/UAT tester: validates the workflow against business intent and records approval or issues.
- Product/BA: resolves requirement ambiguity and scope decisions.
- Release owner: uses UAT outcome as an input to release readiness.

## Exit criteria
- Required business scenarios executed.
- UAT defects triaged.
- Approval or documented exception received.
- Release risks communicated.
