Quality Engineering Framework

Salesforce Testing Strategy

A risk-based manual testing model for Salesforce changes across behavior, roles, data, flows, integrations, regression, UAT, and production validation.

Coverage Model

  • Functional behavior — does the feature meet acceptance criteria?
  • Role & permission behavior — correct behavior for authorized and unauthorized users.
  • Record/data integrity — correct record, field, relationship, ownership, and persistence.
  • Flow paths — positive, negative, conditional, and fault paths.
  • UI behavior — visibility, labels, conditional display, required fields, and navigation.
  • Integration impact — upstream/downstream behavior where applicable.
  • Regression — adjacent workflows that could be affected.
  • UAT readiness — business workflow can be validated in realistic conditions.
  • Production validation — targeted smoke check after release.

Manual QA Principle

Manual testing is treated as structured risk analysis, not ad-hoc clicking. Every test should connect to a business rule, system risk, acceptance criterion, or regression concern.
